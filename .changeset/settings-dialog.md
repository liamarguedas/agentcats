Settings on the board: the default agent, the lead's and workers' agent and model, team size, budgets and captions, saved for the project or as your defaults for every project (`~/.agentcats/agentcats.config.json`). Cats on another agent than their role's default are listed and can be moved, and agentcats says at start when one is.
