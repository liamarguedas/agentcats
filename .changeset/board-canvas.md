The board is a full-screen canvas: pan and zoom the office (drag, scroll, Ctrl or Cmd and scroll, Ctrl or Cmd and + or -, pinch, arrow keys, F or Ctrl 0 to fit), with the chat, tasks and event log in panels on the right and a cat's or task's details on the left. The office is a set of rooms spread over the canvas: the lead's office, the workspace, the meeting room, the break room and, for retired cats, out of office. Each room grows with the team. Names, bubbles and cards stay readable and in view at any zoom, and bubbles move aside instead of covering each other or a cat. **Needs you** in the header and the N key bring the cats waiting for you into view, and markers at the edge point to those out of view. On a phone the panels are bottom sheets.
