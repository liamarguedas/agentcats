# Code of conduct

agentcats is a small, friendly project.
We want everyone who uses it or works on it to feel welcome, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it so it can be accepted.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately, through the email on their GitHub profile.
Reports are read promptly and kept confidential.
The maintainer may warn, remove content, or ban someone from the project, depending on what happened.

Based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
